**Improve effectiveness for longer words**
    Issue: Words may not all appear on doc and do not always appear next to each other
    FIX: Positional indexing ensures that the docs returned contain the words right next to each other
    or as close to each other as available

**Improve effectiveness for common words**
    ISSUE: Documents that are larger and contain the word rank higher despite having little relevance
    and a lower percentage of content related to that word. In code, this is in part to using only
    frequency and idf. 
    FIX: Using tfidf with sublinear tf makes it so that neither long nor short documents dominate.
    Also, positional indexing ensures that the documents returned contain the close next to each other.
